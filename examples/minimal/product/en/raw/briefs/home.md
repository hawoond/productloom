# Storefront Home

## Problem

Customers need a clear starting point for discovering available products.

## Desired Outcome

Customers can understand the current assortment and open a product with minimal effort.

## Requirements

- Show a meaningful loading state.
- Explain when no products are available.
- Keep failures recoverable.
